# Indicative graded response playbook (inspired by graded response action plans; local orders prevail)

This is an indicative playbook for decision support. Actual measures must follow the orders of the competent local authority.

## Stage 1 - Poor air quality
- Strengthen dust control: water sprinkling and mechanical sweeping of roads, covering construction material.
- Enforce rules against open burning of waste and biomass.
- Enforce vehicle emission (pollution-under-control) checks and reduce visible smoke from diesel generators.
- Issue public advisories for sensitive groups.

## Stage 2 - Very poor air quality
- Increase parking fees or restrict parking near congestion hotspots to discourage private vehicle use.
- Increase frequency of public transport and encourage staggered office hours.
- Restrict diesel generator sets except for essential services.
- Intensify enforcement on industrial emissions and brick kilns.

## Stage 3 - Severe air quality
- Pause non-essential construction and demolition work.
- Restrict entry of heavy diesel trucks into the urban core and reroute transit freight around the city.
- Advise schools to move outdoor activities indoors.

## Stage 4 - Severe-plus emergency
- Consider stopping truck entry except essential goods, closing schools, and work-from-home advisories.
- Throttle or temporarily curtail high-emission industrial units in consultation with the pollution control board.

## Traffic rerouting principles
Divert through-traffic and freight away from residential and school zones; prioritise signal timing on arterial roads to reduce idling; promote public transport during episodes.
