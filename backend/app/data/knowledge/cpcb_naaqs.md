# CPCB NAAQS and National AQI (summary)

Note: retrieval summary for this platform. Verify against the current CPCB notification.

## National Ambient Air Quality Standards (ug/m3 unless stated)
- PM2.5: annual 40; 24-hour 60.
- PM10: annual 60; 24-hour 100.
- NO2: annual 40; 24-hour 80.
- SO2: annual 50; 24-hour 80.
- CO: 8-hour 2 mg/m3; 1-hour 4 mg/m3.
- O3: 8-hour 100; 1-hour 180.

## National AQI categories
- 0-50 Good: minimal impact.
- 51-100 Satisfactory: minor breathing discomfort to sensitive people.
- 101-200 Moderate: breathing discomfort to people with lung disease such as asthma, and to children and older adults.
- 201-300 Poor: breathing discomfort on prolonged exposure; illness risk for people with heart disease.
- 301-400 Very Poor: respiratory illness on prolonged exposure.
- 401-500 Severe: affects healthy people and seriously affects those with existing diseases.

## PM sub-index breakpoints (24-hour, ug/m3)
PM2.5: 0-30 Good, 31-60 Satisfactory, 61-90 Moderate, 91-120 Poor, 121-250 Very Poor, 250+ Severe.
PM10: 0-50 Good, 51-100 Satisfactory, 101-250 Moderate, 251-350 Poor, 351-430 Very Poor, 430+ Severe.
