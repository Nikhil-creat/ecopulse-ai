# WHO Global Air Quality Guidelines 2021 (summary)

Note: this is a retrieval summary written for this platform. Verify values against the official WHO publication before using them in regulatory or clinical contexts.

The 2021 update tightened most guideline levels compared with the 2005 edition, reflecting evidence of health effects at lower concentrations.

## Guideline levels (ug/m3 unless stated)
- PM2.5: annual mean 5; 24-hour mean 15.
- PM10: annual mean 15; 24-hour mean 45.
- NO2: annual mean 10; 24-hour mean 25.
- O3: peak-season 8-hour mean 60; 8-hour mean 100.
- SO2: 24-hour mean 40.
- CO: 24-hour mean 4 mg/m3.

The 24-hour levels are the 99th percentile, meaning they may be exceeded only about 3-4 days per year.

## Interim targets
WHO also defines interim targets (IT-1 to IT-4) as stepping stones for polluted regions. For PM2.5 the annual interim targets are 35, 25, 15 and 10 ug/m3 and the 24-hour interim targets are 75, 50, 37.5 and 25 ug/m3.

## Health relevance
Fine particles (PM2.5) penetrate deep into the lungs and bloodstream and are linked with cardiovascular and respiratory disease, asthma exacerbations, and adverse pregnancy outcomes. NO2 is associated with airway inflammation and reduced lung function, particularly in children and people with asthma.
