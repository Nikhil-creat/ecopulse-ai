Know the air before you step into it.

EcoPulse AI forecasts urban air quality up to 72 hours ahead, reads satellite imagery with a CNN, and explains what it means for your health and for city policy.

What it does

  • Forecasts pollutionPM2.5, PM10, NO₂, AQI and temperature at +6 to +72 hours. Three models compete on a 24-hour hold-out and the best one is used. Retraining runs every 3 hours and whenever data drift is detected.
  • Reads the city from aboveA PyTorch CNN scores smog and green cover in satellite or aerial images and draws them as a 3D heatmap.
  • Checks its own dataAn integrity agent flags stale, flat-lined or implausible sensors before any advice is produced.
  • Assesses risk and suggests actionAgents compare readings with WHO and CPCB limits, rate the risk, and propose graded measures for traffic, industry and construction. A person must approve every measure.
  • Answers health questionsA streaming chatbot combines guideline search with live readings and the forecast, and cites its sources.
  • Produces a reportOne click exports an Executive Health & Policy Report as a PDF.

Run it yourself

This page is a showcase. The platform itself needs Docker (a Linux machine or GitHub Codespaces works).

git clone https://github.com/Nikhil-creat/ecopulse-ai
cd ecopulse-ai
cp .env.example .env     # set POSTGRES_PASSWORD, SECRET_KEY, API_KEYS
make up
./scripts/bootstrap.sh http://localhost YOUR_API_KEY

Without API keys it uses clearly labelled simulated data. The CNN uses heuristic scoring until trained on real imagery. Output is decision support, not medical advice.
